CONVERSATION REPAIR SCRIPT SHEET

Use when you missed a commitment or had an emotional spike.

Template:
1) Responsibility: "I missed [specific thing]."
2) Impact: "I know this affected you by [impact]."
3) Repair now: "I will do [specific next action] by [time]."
4) Prevention: "I am adding [system] so this is less likely again."

Examples:
- "I missed paying the bill. I know that added stress. I will pay it by 6pm today and set recurring reminders right after."
- "I snapped in our conversation. I am sorry. I need a 20-minute reset and then I will come back to listen and repair."
