DOPAMINE MENU BUILDER

Goal: Use fast, safe resets before doom scrolling or impulse actions.

QUICK STARTERS (2-5 MIN)
- Stretch or shake for 2 minutes
- Cold water splash
- 1 song dance break
- Step outside

MIDSIZE RESETS (10-20 MIN)
- Short walk
- Shower reset
- Tidy one zone
- Call/text a friend

HEAVY RESETS (30+ MIN)
- Gym or long walk
- Creative project sprint
- Meal prep
- Nature break

EMERGENCY INTERRUPTERS
- Lock shopping apps for 30 minutes
- Put phone in another room
- Body double with someone
- Run 10-minute panic-proof checklist
